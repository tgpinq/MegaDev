How to classify a report

Deduplicate overlapping export rows by report ID. Resolve the tenant from src/lib/data/tenants/, use its locale’s date order, then inspect its form version and the report channel. The implementation is causeOf in src/lib/kb/causes.ts.

A day-first tenant’s booking-v3 form report belongs to hint; its app or email report belongs to parser under the current model. A booking-v2 report from a day-first tenant belongs to parser. An unknown tenant or unsupported case stays unplaced for investigation; do not force it into the closest subject.

Browser locale is not tenant locale. meta.intakeTag is an intake guess, not a finding. The table shows where those guesses disagree with classification.
