0003: confirm cause-based triage with course provenance

owner tgpinq accepted reviewed 2026-10-30 · 0 days ago

Supersedes: 0002: duplicate reports share an evidenced cause. Accepted decisions 0001 and 0002 remain unchanged as history.

Context

Decision 0002 correctly separated causes, but its signature was the author’s GitHub handle. The course requires its issued ticket as the page signature. This replacement records that provenance without editing an accepted decision. The incident review now uses the same issued signature; its render-time desk reference follows from that exact value.

Decision

Retain the cause-based rule from decision 0002. Merge only when the evidence supports a shared cause and compatible affected product and path. Deduplicate report IDs, resolve tenant locale and form version, inspect the channel, and read the body for contradictions. Use causeOf and sameCause for the date cases, not subject matching, browser locale, or intake’s guessed tags.

Unknown tenants, unsupported cases, and timing anomalies stay visible for investigation rather than being forced into a cause. Preserve report IDs and ticket links when reclassifying. Continue routing supported cases while engineering investigates uncertainty.
