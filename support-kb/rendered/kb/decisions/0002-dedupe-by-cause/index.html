0002: duplicate reports share an evidenced cause

owner tgpinq accepted reviewed 2026-10-30 · 0 days ago

Supersedes: 0001: duplicates share a subject line. That accepted record remains unchanged as history. This decision replaces its subject-only rule for the KB’s current triage procedure.

Context

The review of incident #4471 finds distinct parser and date-hint causes in a ticket described as a single known parser bug. Subjects and intake tags do not establish which cause a report belongs to. The old decision and its triage procedure were last reviewed in June 2024; their accepted status does not make their assertions current.

Decision

Merge reports only when the evidence supports a shared cause and compatible affected product and path. First preserve and deduplicate report IDs. For the date cases, use the existing causeOf and sameCause functions: resolve tenant locale, tenant form version, and channel. Read the body for contradictions.

Do not substitute browser locale, guessed intake tags, matching subjects, or the size of a ticket for that evidence. If tenant or cause is unknown, or the body does not fit the model, keep the case for investigation rather than forcing a merge. Revisit the model when evidence changes.

  • App and email reports for kettle.books have the same parser cause despite different subjects.
  • Form and app reports for alder.physio can have the same subject but different causes: booking-v3’s hint versus the shared parser.
  • Form reports for copper.studio and morrow.tax can have the same subject and channel but different causes because their form versions differ.
  • Form reports for pale.harbour and alder.physio share the hint cause despite different subjects.
  • Matching subjects from unknown.sender provide no known cause. Keep them unplaced until investigated.

Consequences

Triage needs tenant lookup and evidence review instead of subject matching alone. This costs attention but avoids hiding a second defect under the first defect’s fix. Retain the original report IDs and ticket links when splitting; count each report once.

The desk can keep receiving and routing reports while engineering investigates the separate causes. Historical classification is a starting point, not proof that every report is fully explained. See the review’s explicit anomalies, ownership needs, and limits on production actions.
